# Causal MatPhys graph-part residual

Run date: 2026-07-18

Status: source gate failed; model family frozen. No wider run and no SOTA claim.

## Motivation

The released PhysTwin trajectory is a much stronger center than an absolute
MatPhys spring prediction, while MatPhys's published ablation identifies its
part decomposition as important. This experiment therefore keeps every
released PhysTwin spring and lets a video-conditioned model predict only a
bounded spatial ratio:

```text
log(k_ij) = log(k_ij^PhysTwin) + log(2) * tanh(r_ij)
```

The maximum change is a factor of two in either direction. Released damping,
collision, and contact parameters stay fixed.

The local released Bayesian-PhysTwin selected stack is approximately
`10.17 mm` CD and `19.21 mm` manual-track error. The published MatPhys point is
approximately `8/15 mm` on the same headline metrics. Closing that external
gap requires about a `21%` to `22%` reduction in both metrics. Passing the source
gate below is therefore only a competence check, not evidence that this method
has reached or exceeded the published frontier.

## Causal input contract

Legacy MatPhys experiments in this repository used lexicographically sorted,
unpadded PNG names. Values such as `10.png` were therefore treated as earlier
than `2.png`, and all 22 schema-1 audits included at least one frame beyond the
declared evidence boundary. Those runs remain frozen engineering negatives,
but they are not future-blind evidence and their adaptive family gains are not
causal results. The exact filename reconstruction is archived in
`results/sota/matphys_legacy_frame_order_audit_v1.json`; all 22 cases cross
their declared boundary.

Audit schema 2 fails closed on every schema-1 checkpoint. It binds:

- numeric filename frame IDs rather than list positions;
- exact image paths and SHA-256 hashes;
- a fit-only physical objective and terminal checkpoint;
- a disjoint validation suffix inside the released observation prefix;
- the exact DINO, depth, mask, calibration, and graph-part artifacts.

No future RGB, depth, mask, track, or object geometry may enter fitting or
family selection. Known future controller targets remain part of the official
action-conditioned PhysTwin rollout task.

## Public part approximation

The public MatPhys repository does not include its generated Gaussian-part
artifacts. The replacement is intentionally labeled
`causal-dino-graph-voronoi-parts-v1`, not a MatPhys reproduction:

1. Four fixed numeric keyframes are selected before the fit boundary.
2. DINOv2 descriptors are sampled at tracked material points in every
   calibrated view.
3. Object masks and metric depth reject occluded projections.
4. Unobserved surface and interior nodes inherit only their nearest observed
   material descriptor.
5. Farthest-point seeds and semantic-geometric graph distances produce five
   connected Voronoi parts.

The released simple MatPhys decoder loads part descriptors but does not use
them. An opt-in zero-initialized adapter projects each DINO descriptor into the
per-part material embedding. Setting its scale to zero reproduces the upstream
decoder; setting the teacher residual scale to zero reproduces the exact
released PhysTwin spring field.

## Paired family gate

Every export runs two fresh official Warp trajectories in the same process:

- the learned bounded graph-part residual;
- the exact released-PhysTwin teacher.

Only prefix-truncated copies ending at the released training boundary are
available to `bpt-gate-matphys-part-family`. The gate scores the untouched
suffix `[fit_end, train_end)` and selects the learned family only when its
balanced score improves and neither CD nor manual-track error regresses. Ties
fall back to the teacher. The gate never loads or scores the future trajectory.

The paired scale-zero control exposed a replay-floor edge case before source
validation: it appeared to improve the balanced score by `0.00070%` despite
identical physical parameters. The prospective amendment in
`configs/sota/matphys_graph_parts_source_v1_amendment.json` therefore requires
at least `0.1%` balanced improvement. All model, data, and no-regression choices
remain unchanged.

## CUDA smoke

On `single_lift_sloth`, a scale-zero candidate and its exact-teacher control
generated by the paired exporter differ by only `0.00056 mm` on average over
all node-frames (`0.00131 mm` on the validation suffix and `0.00079 mm` in the
future). A two-epoch positive-scale smoke updates the DINO adapter but mostly
learns a global spring ratio around `1.315`; its five part means span only
`0.00165`. The disjoint gate correctly rejects it: validation CD is `11.24%`
worse and validation track error is `12.94%` worse than the paired exact
teacher. No future metric was opened for this decision.

## Frozen source panel

The first normal training run is locked in
`configs/sota/matphys_graph_parts_source_v1.json`: one canonical single-lift
case for each released material label, one shared 20-epoch checkpoint, fixed
five-part construction, and the unchanged factor-two residual bound. At least
three of five learned cases and both selected-stack aggregate validation
metrics must beat the all-teacher stack before any wider exploratory run is
admitted.

Even a passing source gate is not SOTA evidence. The 22 released PhysTwin cases
have already been examined repeatedly. A defensible SOTA claim requires a
fresh, independently locked cohort with complete PhysTwin twins and the same
unaltered family gate.

### Frozen outcome

The terminal epoch-20 checkpoint passed its schema-2 causal audit, but the
learned family was accepted on only `2/5` source cases, below the locked `3/5`
requirement. The wider released cohort was therefore not run and no future
metric was opened.

| Source case | Candidate CD change | Candidate track change | Decision |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `single_lift_sloth` | -8.92% | -6.33% | learned |
| `single_lift_zebra` | +11.42% | +7.28% | teacher |
| `single_lift_cloth` | +11.35% | +10.93% | teacher |
| `single_lift_rope` | +36.46% | +40.55% | teacher |
| `single_lift_dinosor` | -59.83% | -39.30% | learned |

The selected stack improves aggregate validation CD from `43.795 mm` to
`21.780 mm` and track error from `64.570 mm` to `45.497 mm`, but that aggregate
is dominated by the unusually poor exact-teacher dinosaur trajectory. It does
not override the predeclared case-count gate.

The mechanism diagnostic is decisive: every case reaches an approximately
`0.5x` stiffness ratio, the exact lower bound, and the largest range among the
five part means is only `1.12e-7`. The model learned uniform spring softening,
not material-aware parts. This rejects the combination of a hard factor-two
`tanh` residual and the current shared decoder on this source panel.

## Next admissible family

Do not widen the residual cap or tune this family on the opened source suffix.
The next defensible hypothesis is a low-dimensional hierarchical model:

```text
log(k_e) = log(k_e^PhysTwin) + alpha_case + beta_part[e]
alpha_case ~ Normal(mu_material, sigma_material)
beta_part ~ Normal(0, tau_material)
```

Use continuous offsets with learned shrinkage scales rather than a saturated
hard cap, retain the exact-teacher fallback, and fit on a newly declared source
panel. This tests whether the data identify material-level and part-level
softening separately. A spatial neural field or topology change is justified
only if the low-dimensional part model first demonstrates out-of-sample
transfer.
