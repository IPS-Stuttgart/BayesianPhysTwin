# Joint DLO4/DLO5 source-replication synthesis

This protocol adds a complete-trajectory, DLO-stratified synthesis of the two
already completed source panels from protected run `33361441865`.

The analysis was frozen after the DLO4 and DLO5 source results were available
but while the target job was still in `Refit and predict both fresh DLOs in
parallel`, before a joint prediction seal or target score existed. It therefore
cannot increase the independence of the target result. Its purpose is narrower:
replace two isolated source-gate statements with one reproducible joint
source-replication statistic.

The evaluator reports pooled mean coordinate L1, all trajectory-level
wins/ties/losses, a DLO-stratified complete-trajectory bootstrap interval, the
free-node diagnostic, and equal-DLO source uncertainty summaries. The original
per-DLO gates remain the only decision rule; the new bootstrap is descriptive
and does not retroactively select a method.

A positive result supports consistent source-side replication across DLO4 and
DLO5. It is not held-out target evidence, a DLO-population generalization
p-value, cross-backend transfer, arbitrary-object generalization, safety, or
state of the art.
