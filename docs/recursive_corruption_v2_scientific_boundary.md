# Scientific boundary

A favorable v2 result establishes only controlled synthetic evidence that
Gaussian recursive belief propagation adds value beyond a deterministic residual
method with the same guard under the exact registered varying domains and
corruption families.

It does not establish real-provider competence, physical-object or
physical-session transfer, physical-data uncertainty calibration, a latent
physical-state interpretation, Causal4D intervention benefit, deployment safety,
or general state of the art. Downstream evidence cannot rescue a failed v2
criterion, and v2 cannot rescue an unopened real-provider or physical gate.
