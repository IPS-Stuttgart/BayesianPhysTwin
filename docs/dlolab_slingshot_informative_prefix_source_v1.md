# Native Slingshot: Early Informative Pull Source Screen

This is a new source-development question after the frozen 32-world belief
study failed. That result and every DEFORM result remain unchanged. No new
recording, robot, GPU, calibration/evaluation payload, or protected target is
used. Only the already-open 27-world model grid and public native assets are
used; no result here is independent method confirmation or benchmark SOTA.

The late-prefix source audit found much more stretching information after
macro 2, but too little remaining action value. This screen instead moves
25% or 50% of the incumbent's macro-2 translation into macro 1, subtracting
the same vector from macro 2 for every candidate. It preserves three macros,
their summed translation commands, all rotations, and the seven candidate
identities. It does not assume identical physical endpoints: timing can change
contact and material response. Translation norms remain at most 0.1 m and
angle components at most 1 rad. No projection or clipping silently changes
these two fixed probes. Native reward, physics, controller, and release remain
unchanged. This is a changed control policy, not a new simulator mechanism.

## Prefix Screen

Each probe runs only to frame 299 on the nine existing x=0 material-grid
worlds. Two eight-slot batches cover nine worlds; padding is never counted as
additional evidence. Rod nodes 3/6/8 and the sphere center at frames
139/219/299 are the same 12 observations as before. Noise stays 2 mm independent
xyz plus one 5 mm shared xyz bias. No future rollout or new-probe reward is
available during probe selection.

All prefix arrays must be finite, fixed endpoints within 1e-9 m, and padded
duplicate positions within 0.5 mm. A candidate needs a half-range stretching
secant norm at least 1 after whitening by the registered full observation
covariance. The greater such norm is selected, ties favoring the smaller probe.
Both probes must pass native QA. Bending sensitivity is reported, not silently
assumed identified. This threshold is an engineering information screen, not
a calibrated probability, global observability theorem, or performance test.

## Conditional Source Value

Only a rederived passing prefix record permits source futures. The selected
probe runs once on each of the 27 already-open model-grid worlds, with seven
actions and one exact incumbent duplicate. Full prefixes replay the separately
generated x=0 prefixes within 0.5 mm. Common-prefix, duplicate, endpoint and
reward QA use the existing native contracts. Fresh processes replace hidden
state restart. Failures are terminal and retained, with no replacement/retry.

The source screen integrates the ideal discrete-model Bayes decision under
8192 Gaussian observation draws per model world, seed 260907, with common
draws for paired numerical integration. Tensor prior weights stay
`[0.25,0.5,0.25]^3`. Compare the posterior mean with the strongest blind action,
the MAP particle action, and perfect information. Numerical standard errors
are Monte Carlo integration error, not experimental confidence intervals.

Advancement requires all 27 source worlds, posterior information gain at least
0.005 and at least 10% of the new best blind action's reward above zero, gain
over MAP at least 0.002, and posterior reward no lower than the original
unmodified source bank's strongest blind action. All comparisons use native
reward, not a renamed or rescaled objective. Even a passing source screen
requires a distinct frozen continuous-world calibration/evaluation study;
it does not automatically authorize one.
