# Slingshot Active Bayesian Identification Source Protocol

## Status And Question

This is a distinct prospective public-simulator source study generated by one
post-result observation from the failed 27-particle screen: posterior-integrated
control improved under the finite prior while a plug-in MAP material decision
regressed. The parent gate remains failed, its root remains terminal, and its
0.005 threshold is not changed.

The new primary question is narrower:

> On fresh continuous material and placement worlds, does a posterior Bayes
> action after an active identification maneuver beat the action selected from
> one MAP material particle, as well as blind and passive Bayesian controls?

The Bayesian update and expected-utility decision are standard. A positive
result would be controlled source evidence for propagating material uncertainty
into deformable-object decisions, not a new theorem, real-world result, or SOTA
claim.

## Fresh Worlds And Sensing

Freeze 32 new continuous worlds with NumPy seed 261301. Placement is uniform in
`[-0.02,0.02]` m; native bending and stretching parameters are log-uniform over
the same ranges covered by the sealed 27-particle bank. Exact tuple comparison
must show no overlap with the particle grid or either earlier continuous
calibration/evaluation roster.

For every world, generate two prefix-only native episodes from fresh processes:
the original passive prefix and the 70% frontloaded identification prefix. The
active maneuver was selected in prior development; it is not searched here.
Both expose only frames 139, 219, and 299 for rod nodes 3, 6, and 8 plus the
sphere center. No reward or task future is available at this stage.

Use eight registered synthetic sensor draws per world with seed 261302. Each
draw has 2 mm independent xyz noise and a shared 5 mm xyz bias; the same draw is
applied to passive and active prefixes. Eight draws reduce sensor Monte Carlo
noise but do not create eight physical replicates. Decisions and rewards are
averaged within each world, and the world is the sole statistical unit.

## Matched-reset Semantics And Arms

The identification maneuver is a separate episode. The task starts from a new
native reset with the same world parameters, so probing does not receive credit
for direct task progress. Probe time or energy is not included in the native task
reward; the claim is therefore active system identification before a resettable
task, not free online dual control.

Use the unchanged 27-particle passive/active history bank, prior weights,
seven-action reward bank, and likelihood. Compare:

1. prior-optimal blind action;
2. passive-prefix plug-in MAP action;
3. passive-prefix posterior Bayes action;
4. active-prefix plug-in MAP action;
5. active-prefix posterior Bayes action (primary).

For MAP, choose the reward-optimal action of the maximum-posterior particle. For
Bayes, maximize posterior expected reward across all particles. No continuous
parameter fitting, action optimization, guard calibration, or outcome-dependent
fallback is permitted.

## Custody And Execution

Generate and seal all eight native prefix batches, observations, posterior
weights, and 256 decisions per arm before any task future starts. The pre-future
barrier requires all native prefix checks, at least 32 nonblind active-Bayes
sensor decisions, at least 16 active-Bayes/MAP disagreements, and at least two
distinct active-Bayes actions. These checks use no task outcome.

Only after that barrier, run one fresh native seven-action table plus an exact
duplicate action for each world. Every future must replay the separately sealed
passive prefix within 0.5 mm, preserve fixed endpoints within 1e-9 m, match the
duplicate trajectory within 0.5 mm and duplicate reward within 0.001, and bind
the exact world and decision barrier. Any failure is retained with no retry or
replacement.

## Frozen Source Gate

Average the eight sensor decisions inside each world, then use equal-weighted
world means. Use 20,000 paired world-bootstrap replicates with seed 261303.
Require all 32 worlds and native checks; at least three oracle actions; active
Bayes gain of at least 0.003 over blind, 0.001 over passive Bayes, and 0.005 over
active MAP; paired 95% lower bounds above zero for those three comparisons; at
least 20% of observed oracle headroom; and no more world-level harms beyond the
0.002 numerical margin than active MAP.

Report every arm, all paired intervals, action frequencies, oracle-action rate,
harm counts, and complete failure accounting. A failed gate remains informative
about finite-bank transfer but promotes nothing. No threshold, roster, sensor
draw, action, or arm may change after any future is opened.

## Boundaries

This study is CPU-only and uses the public native Genesis ROD simulator. It uses
no new recording, GPU, protected target, held-v8 artifact, DLO4/DLO5 data,
official DLO3 evaluation, PokeFlex continuation, or fresh/reserved Deform360
data. It cannot alter successful DEFORM evidence, reclassify the parent gate,
claim automatic perception, price the identification maneuver, establish real
material parameters, or authorize a public benchmark/SOTA claim.
