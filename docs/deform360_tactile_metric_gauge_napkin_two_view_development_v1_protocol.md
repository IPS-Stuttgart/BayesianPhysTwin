# Napkin two-view metric-gauge development protocol

The independent cake case stopped before the metric-gauge stage because its
robot prefix failed the unchanged source gate. This development-only run asks
whether the already-frozen conservative two-view gauge is executable on the
previously opened napkin geometry that originally exposed the two-camera
regime.

The policy was fixed before any napkin MotionCrafter array was opened. Candidate
scope is limited to the three archived Stage 1 provider cameras. Target-free
contact projection selects `brics-odroid-019_cam1` and
`brics-odroid-022_cam1`: both have full assignment coverage, margins above 64
pixels, and a 93.04 degree view separation. No supplemental provider is run.

Both direct and swapped tactile assignments must pass the unchanged 5 mm
median and 15 mm 90th-percentile held-frame errors in both cameras. Each camera
uses a 10 mm covariance floor. Unknown cross-view correlation produces no
precision gain; the reported covariance union dominates both camera
covariances and adds a separate 10 mm shared-bias floor.

This case motivated the two-view model class and is not independent evidence.
Admission can establish only development feasibility and authorize a separate
carrier-admission diagnostic. It cannot authorize opening prediction scores,
claiming transfer, or claiming SOTA performance. Failure is exact fallback.
