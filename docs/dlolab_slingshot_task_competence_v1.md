# Native Slingshot: Bounded Task-Competence Search

This is a development/source control-bank experiment, not a Bayesian test or a
published-controller reproduction. Passing fresh-process native qualification
is required and rederived from all sealed numeric/boolean arrays before launch.
The reused-reset failure stays frozen. Existing DEFORM evidence is untouched.

Exactly 24 actions are fixed before execution: the zero and simple pull controls;
18 combinations of simultaneous/lift-then-pull profiles, lifts -10/20/50 mm and
total pulls 30/60/90 mm; and four fixed-seed pull-biased random 18-DOF controls.
Every action respects the published 100 mm per-stage translation and 1 radian
per-component rotation limits. No action is chosen using a future outcome or
adaptively revised during this bank. Each runs in a fresh CPU process through
the unchanged native robot, contact model, controller, release, and reward.

All 24 candidates remain in accounting, including technical/physical failures.
No failed candidate is retried. Trace arrays and full native memory are sealed
before scores are assembled. Native float32 cumulative reward is independently
recomputed from the 30 exact readout instants, including the post-release final
frame. A candidate is useful only if sphere and cube advance at least 10 mm,
reward improves by at least 0.01 over the exact native zero control, and the
tracked gripper remains at least 80 mm from the cube. The last check does not
prove absence of every possible robot-link interaction; it excludes the most
direct end-effector shortcut. All candidates must complete for the bank gate.

The best capable candidate is highest native reward, then lowest index. Success
only permits designing a subsequent source-frozen uncertainty/decision-value
test with strong matched point controls. It does not establish that uncertainty
helps, that a Bayesian controller wins, or that this matches the published
CMA-ES budget. No new recordings, targets, protected outcomes, GPU work, push,
or main merge are allowed by this experiment.
