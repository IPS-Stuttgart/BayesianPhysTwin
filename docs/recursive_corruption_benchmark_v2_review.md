# Recursive-corruption v2 review contract

This document records the mandatory revisions addressed by v2. It is distinct
from the v1 retained evidence and must not silently replace it.

## Mandatory revisions

1. **Matched guard:** compare `guarded_recursive` with
   `guarded_last_residual`, which receives the same availability, reliability,
   reported-timestamp, innovation, trust-region, and fallback information.
2. **Imperfect cues:** include false-negative reliability, false-positive
   reliability, timestamp jitter, partial staleness, and mixed identities.
3. **Co-equal endpoints:** review full, corruption-window, and recovery-window
   RMSE together with clean cost, harmful seed-domains, worst-seed regret, and
   exact fallback.
4. **Reproducible analysis:** unsupported metrics are `null`; uncertainty is
   computed after reducing nested conditions within fresh seed-domains; every
   retained product is regenerated byte-for-byte by `--check`.
5. **Primary endpoint discipline:** exactly two endpoints are primary; all
   remaining contrasts are secondary registered companion evidence.
6. **Trace retention:** per-time-step errors and routing decisions are retained
   in a deterministic trace archive, making recovery curves reproducible.
7. **Fresh heterogeneous roster:** the evidence roster is `100000:100200`,
   disjoint from development tests, and varies dynamics, actions, discrepancy,
   noise, timing, and corruption severity by seed-domain.

## Frozen co-equal decision

Promotion requires all of the following without retuning on the fresh roster:

- upper paired 95% bound below zero for both primary endpoints;
- upper paired 95% bound below zero for corruption-window and recovery-window
  RMSE versus the matched guarded residual arm;
- clean-cost upper bound no larger than 0.100 mm;
- harmful seed-domain fraction no larger than 0.25;
- worst observed mean stress regret no larger than 1.000 mm; and
- zero exact-fallback identity violations.

A failed criterion is retained as a negative result. It cannot be repaired on
the same fresh roster.

## Boundary

This is controlled synthetic mechanism evidence. It does not establish real
provider competence, physical-object/session transfer, physical-data
calibration, physical-state validity, Causal4D benefit, deployment safety, or
state of the art.
