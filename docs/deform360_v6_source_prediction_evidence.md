## Evidence generated

The protected workflow reconstructs the frozen physical fallback and the v5
joint-sparse prefix-only source panel on `workstation2`.

For every one of ten development objects it:

1. verifies the retained processed-source bytes against the authoritative
   calibration-source artifact;
2. reconstructs the frame-zero material cloud from the causal prefix only;
3. constructs the automatic Warp twin when admissible, otherwise exact
   persistence;
4. selects at most eight visual likelihood cameras by a metadata-only SHA-256
   rule from the validated v4 supported-stream roster;
5. excludes the two independently reserved endpoint views;
6. retains the released tactile/robot-axis ambiguity as an explicit no-contact
   disposition;
7. constructs one outer held-out and nine inner cross-fitted predictions; and
8. publishes the complete 100-record source prediction batch atomically.

Every prediction binds its exact other-nine or other-eight fit roster, physical
fallback, visual factors, risk score, source artifact identities, candidate
artifacts, and implementation revision. The source panel preserves the existing
v5 prediction and fallback contracts rather than defining proxy losses for v6.

## Camera policy

Camera selection is frozen before reading a provider point value, residual, or
outcome. For each object, cameras are ranked by

```text
SHA256(object_id || NUL || camera_id || NUL ||
       "v6-source-likelihood-panel-v1")
```

Only streams admitted by the validated v4 metric-prefix plan are eligible. The
two reserved endpoint views are excluded. The first eight remaining cameras are
used, with a minimum of two. A missing minimum is retained as a support or
technical result; no camera or object is replaced.

## Information barrier

This workflow does not:

- open or score the development suffix;

- select a v6 fresh object or episode;
- open a v6 target payload or outcome;
- use a future object observation in prediction;

The complete prediction batch must exist before a later reviewed source-scoring
stage may attach development suffix outcomes. The scoring stage must bind those
outcomes to the exact batch and 100 prediction seals produced here.

## Terminal states

The compact execution receipt records exactly one of:

- `source-prediction-evidence-sealed`: ten physical manifests, 100 immutable
  source prediction seals, one complete prediction batch, and one source
  prediction receipt exist;
- `source-inputs-incomplete`: a required frozen source, model, checkpoint, or
  retained input was unavailable before a valid prediction batch existed;
- `source-technical-failure-retained`: execution reached a registered stage but
  failed to complete it; or
- `invalid`: source identity, runner identity, content identity, or information
  boundary changed.

Missing infrastructure is not interpreted as poor predictive performance. A
successful prediction stage is likewise not a positive source gate: it only
permits the separately reviewed development-suffix scoring stage.
