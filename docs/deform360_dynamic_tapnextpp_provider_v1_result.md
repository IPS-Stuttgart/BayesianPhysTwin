## Information boundary

The first physical smoke exposed two compatibility defects before any provider
outcome. Both were documented as protocol amendments and fixed without changing
cohort membership, the method, or a gate:

1. the physical runner used the generic rather than dynamic admission adapter;
2. the automatic-twin provenance snapshot requested an obsolete protocol key.

At the final revision, all eight source physical backbones ran through the
official Warp path. Provider execution then began under the frozen rules.
Every failure was retained in the denominator without retry or replacement.
Source futures were authorized only after the complete eight-case barrier. No
source assimilation outcome, sealed target artifact, or held-v8 artifact was
opened.

## Diagnosis

V1 placed two prerequisites too late:

1. The camera panel was not required to have every selected prefix depth stream
   before cohort locking.
2. Cohort admission did not require the frozen physical rollout to support a
   complete dynamic-query schedule.

Five objects failed the hard motion-plus-three-view query schedule. Three
others selected at least one camera without `rendered_depth.h5`. Because the
tracker never ran, changing TAPNext++ itself is not the next experiment.

## Successor requirements

Any V2 study should be a new protocol, not a retry of V1:

1. Validate all selected camera RGB, depth, mask, calibration, and authorized
   prefix frames before cohort selection.
2. Build the frame-zero twin and causal physical rollout before locking the
   source/target partition.
3. Require a target-free dynamic-query admission certificate, including
   predicted action response, multiview visibility, and graph-mode coverage.
4. Permit a variable number of births only under a newly frozen source gate;
   do not weaken V1 after observing these failures.
5. Keep three-view observations claim-bearing, use two-view tracks only for
   continuity/proposals, and retain exact baseline fallback.
6. Select and lock genuinely fresh objects after updating the exclusion union.

The V1 target cohort must remain sealed and cannot be used to repair this
provider.
