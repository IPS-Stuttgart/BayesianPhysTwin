# MatPhys plus automatic CoTracker selected-overlay source protocol

## Question

The object-disjoint guarded MatPhys/Bayesian stack improves the released
PhysTwin prior but remains above the published `8/15 mm` operating point. An
automatic CoTracker3 source-depth correction improves the raw MatPhys replay,
while the strongest manual-prefix ceiling crosses both reference values.

This source diagnostic tests the missing composition: apply the unchanged
automatic prefix correction to the already prefix-selected MatPhys/Bayesian
overlay. It asks whether the physical and observation gains are complementary.

## Information boundary

- The cohort is the previously opened PhysTwin-22 development set.
- MatPhys fitting excludes every target physical object.
- MatPhys strength and the Bayesian overlay are selected before future opening.
- CoTracker3 uses RGB-D evidence strictly before each released training boundary.
- Manual trajectories are evaluation-only and cannot alter a prediction.
- The primary temporal/cap arm and fixed-scale control are named before this run.
- The analyzer ignores every other candidate emitted by the historical broad runner.

The selected-overlay baseline is sequential: it already consumed the permitted
prefix once, and the automatic observation layer then models its remaining
prefix residual. This is a development composition, not an independent update
claim.

## Gate

The primary arm must simultaneously:

1. improve equal-case Chamfer and track error by at least 5% versus the selected overlay;
2. improve both metrics in at least 16 of 22 cases;
3. keep every case-metric regression at or below 10%; and
4. fall below both published context values, 8 mm Chamfer and 15 mm track error.

A pass authorizes only a genuinely fresh public-object source panel. Failure
stops this composition without choosing another candidate from the opened
broad report.

## Claim boundary

All 22 outcomes were previously examined. This experiment can establish
mechanism compatibility and locate remaining headroom, but it cannot establish
independent transfer, calibration, or state of the art. It does not modify the
frozen Causal4D or Deform360 protocols.
