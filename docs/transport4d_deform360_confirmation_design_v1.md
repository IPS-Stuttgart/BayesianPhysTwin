# Transport4D Deform360 public confirmation design

## Scientific question

For a previously unseen real Deform360 object and a fixed held action, can a
source-frozen selector determine the strongest reusable part of a learned
physical correction before the held tactile response is opened?

The candidate reuse levels are ordered from strongest to weakest:

1. unchanged correction coefficients;
2. a query-identifiable transported effect;
3. a one-scalar target-local recalibration;
4. uncertainty/dependence only, with no deterministic mean correction;
5. the frozen fitting procedure with a full target-local refit; and
6. no transport, with exact fallback.

The selector is evaluated on the consequences of its tier choice, not merely on
tier-label accuracy.

## Frozen public cohort

The design binds the existing outcome-blind Deform360 reserve and file-identity
support audit:

- 190 metadata-bearing objects were found;
- 120 object namespaces were conservatively protected;
- the remaining 70 were frozen as 23 calibration and 47 confirmation objects;
- carrier qualification retained 15 calibration and 33 confirmation objects;
- support-negative objects remain assigned and cannot be replaced.

The independent statistical unit is one physical object. Frames, windows,
queries, sensors, and tactile cells are nested observations.

## Registered query and information order

The primary target is a 16-dimensional tactile-response query. For each of four
released tactile sensors it contains:

- log positive-response mass;
- active-taxel fraction;
- positive-response row centroid; and
- positive-response column centroid.

The target window starts at frame 16 and is scored at frame 48. Before the joint
prediction seal, the method may use only tactile prefix indices 0, 12, 15, and
16, the registered future robot intervention segment, and tier-specific source
information. The target future tactile response remains scoring-only.

For tiers that permit target-object adaptation, only carrier-complete non-target
episodes may be used. The fixed target episode and action cannot be reselected.
All 33 object predictions, tier choices, source scores, thresholds, policy
identities, and exact fallbacks must be jointly sealed before any target future
response is numerically opened.

## Primary evidence

The primary endpoint is object-balanced normalized RMSE of the registered
16-dimensional query. The principal comparison is against the best single fixed
tier selected on calibration. Additional controls always use exact coefficients,
one-scalar adaptation, uncertainty-only reuse, procedure-only refitting, or
exact fallback. Source-distance, ensemble-disagreement, physics-residual
disagreement, and deterministic random gates are compared at matched coverage.
A hindsight tier oracle is diagnostic only.

A successful selector must:

- score every supported confirmation object or retain it as support-negative;
- introduce no replacement, prediction-seal, or exact-fallback violation;
- improve mean object loss over exact fallback and over the best calibration-
  selected fixed tier;
- have a paired object-bootstrap upper endpoint below zero against that fixed
  tier;
- pass the registered one-sided object sign test;
- select at least two distinct nonfallback tiers; and
- use no target threshold changes, model changes, or scientific retry.

The guarded-transport risk claim is separate. It requires at least 29 accepted
objects, zero practically harmful accepted transfers, and an exact one-sided 95%
Clopper--Pearson upper endpoint no larger than 10%. With zero harms, the upper
endpoint is 9.81% at 29 accepted objects and 8.68% at all 33.

## Claim boundary

This file freezes an evaluation design; it does not bind a passing calibration
policy or authorize confirmation access. Public logs cannot establish an
individual counterfactual for an unexecuted action from the identical physical
state, online probe-then-act behavior, or robot safety. A later authorization
must bind an explicitly positive terminal calibration result and the complete
source-frozen policy without changing this design.
