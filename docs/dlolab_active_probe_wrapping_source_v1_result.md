null, tension, compression, or axial-shear excursion. Native realized gripper
positions at that endpoint differ by **0.568805 mm**, exceeding the locked
**0.010 mm** maximum. The gate therefore fails.

times the preregistered equivalence budget. It reinforces the project's distinction
between commanded and measured actuation. It does **not** show that active probing
is ineffective: no information or decision outcome was computed.
