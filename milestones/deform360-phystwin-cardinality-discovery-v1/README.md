independent `002-rope-silk` protocol. It is a method-development milestone, not
a benchmark claim.

1. `two-action-exploratory.json`: unimanual episodes 1 and 4 favored separate
   action-response and autonomous-drift trust.

   trust rule.

   aggregate action-response weight.

   action-response weight by registered controller count repaired the episode-6
   transfer and became the explicitly post-hoc hypothesis for independent
   validation.

`configs/causal4d_public/deform360_cardinality_trust_002_rope_silk_v1.json`.
